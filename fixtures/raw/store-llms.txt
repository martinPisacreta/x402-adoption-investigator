## translate-es

Translate to Spanish (Argentina): rioplatense voseo, any source language
Translate text to Spanish as spoken in Argentina and Uruguay (rioplatense, voseo): up to 6,000 characters from any language, keeping names, numbers, links and formatting. Use this when an agent needs Spanish copy for Argentine or Uruguayan users, such as support replies, product listings, notifications or marketing, instead of neutral or European Spanish. Optional tone: formal, neutral or casual. JSON output. Free sample at /api/translate-es/sample.
